The game may store files in your browser cache so it can load faster and work offline. This cache stays on your device and can be cleared from your browser settings.

This version of Garden Match does not include analytics, advertising networks, tracking pixels, or third-party marketing scripts.

Garden Match is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
